An NDE follow-up tool, started with one macro. (1) In the NDE Report (Weld Seam No, Method, Result, Report Date) write Repair in an Action column for every Fail and clear it on the others. (2) On a Repairs sheet list the Weld Seam No and Method of every Fail, with headings. (3) In the Weld Log (Weld Seam No, Welder ID) write the Result from the NDE Report in an NDE Result column for each seam, or leave it blank when the seam has no NDE row. (4) On a Method Summary sheet write, for each NDE method, the number of reports and the number of fails. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
