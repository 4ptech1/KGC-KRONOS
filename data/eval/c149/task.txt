A welder qualification audit, started with one macro. (1) In Welder Quals (Welder ID, Process, Expiry Date, Remark) write Expired in Remark where the qualification has expired, Expires soon where it expires in the next 30 days, and clear it on the others. (2) In the Weld Log (Weld Seam No, Welder ID, Process, Weld Date, Qual Check) write Not qualified in Qual Check when the welder has no qualification for that process that was still valid on the Weld Date, and clear it on the others. (3) On a Welder Problems sheet write each Welder ID with its number of Not qualified welds, the most first. (4) Tell me how many welds are Not qualified. IDs and processes differ in case and stray spaces. The output sheet is rebuilt every run. Each numbered step should also be a macro I can run on its own.
