A job quality tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Job Tracker (Job ID, Customer, Status, Open NCRs) write in Open NCRs how many Open NCRs each job has in the NCR Register (NCR No, Job ID, Status), 0 when none. (2) On a Jobs On Hold sheet list the jobs that are not Closed and have at least one Open NCR, with the same headings, the most NCRs first. (3) On an Unmatched NCRs sheet list the NCRs whose Job ID is not in the Job Tracker, with the NCR Register's headings. (4) Tell me how many jobs are on hold. Job IDs differ in case and stray spaces. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
