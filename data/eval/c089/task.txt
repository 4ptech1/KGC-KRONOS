A job material tool, started with one macro. (1) In the BOM sheet (Job ID, Part No, Required Qty, Issued Qty, Remark) write Short in Remark where Issued Qty is less than Required Qty, Check where either quantity is not a number, and clear Remark on the others. (2) On a Job Shortage sheet write each Job ID that has Short lines with its number of Short lines, the most first. (3) In the Job Tracker (Job ID, Customer, Status, Material Flag) write Waiting material in Material Flag for every job that is not Closed and has at least one Short line, and clear it on the others. (4) Tell me how many jobs are waiting. The Job Shortage sheet is rebuilt every run.
