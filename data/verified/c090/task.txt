A calibration follow-up tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Calibration Tracker (Instrument ID, Type, Location, Next Calibration Due) highlight the whole row red when the instrument is overdue and yellow when it is due in the next 30 days, clearing old highlights first. (2) On a Due List sheet list the overdue and due-soon instruments with the same headings, the earliest due date first. (3) On a Location Summary sheet write each Location with how many of its instruments are overdue and how many are due soon, locations A to Z. (4) Tell me how many are overdue. Instruments with no due date are left alone. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
