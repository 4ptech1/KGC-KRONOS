A calibration follow-up tool, started with one macro, with these parts in order. (1) In the Calibration Tracker (Instrument ID, Type, Location, Next Calibration Due), highlight the whole row red when overdue and yellow when due in the next 30 days, clearing old highlights first. (2) Due List sheet: the overdue and due-soon instruments with the same headings, earliest due date first. (3) Location Summary sheet: each Location with how many of its instruments are overdue and how many are due soon, locations A to Z. (4) Tell me how many instruments are overdue. Instruments with no due date are left alone. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
