A dispatch review tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Dispatch sheet (Dispatch No, Job ID, Customer, Planned Date, Actual Date) write Late in a Remark column when the Actual Date is after the Planned Date, and clear the Remark on the others. (2) On a Late Dispatches sheet copy every Late row with its headings. (3) In the Job Tracker (Job ID, Status) write Dispatched in a Dispatch Check column for every job that has a dispatch row with an Actual Date, and clear it on the others. (4) On a Dispatch Summary sheet write the number of dispatches, late dispatches and dispatched jobs, with labels. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
