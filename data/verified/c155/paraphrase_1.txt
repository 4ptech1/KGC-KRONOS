Weld NDE follow-up tool, one macro to start. (1) Weld Log (Weld Seam No, Welder ID, NDE Status): NDE Status = Rejected if the NDE Report (Weld Seam No, Method, Result) has a Reject for that seam, Accepted if it has rows and none is Reject, Not tested if none. (2) Repair List sheet: Rejected welds, same headings, Weld Seam No A to Z. (3) Welder Rejects sheet: each Welder ID with its number of Rejected welds, most first. (4) Report welds Not tested. Seam numbers differ in case and stray spaces. Output sheets rebuilt every run. Each numbered step also a macro on its own.
