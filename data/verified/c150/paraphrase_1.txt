Receiving tool, one macro to start. (1) GRN (GRN No, Part No, Heat No, Received Qty, Remark): Remark = No cert if Heat No has no row in Material Certs (Heat No, MTC No), Check if Received Qty is not a positive number, else cleared. (2) BOM (Part No, Required Qty, Received, Status): Received = total received per part over all GRN lines; Status = Complete if at least Required Qty, else Short. (3) Short Parts sheet: Short BOM lines, same headings, Part No A to Z. (4) Report parts Short. Part and heat numbers differ in case and stray spaces. Output sheet rebuilt every run. Each numbered step also a macro on its own.
