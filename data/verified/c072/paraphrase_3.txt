Inspection follow-up tool, started with one macro; the output sheets are rebuilt every run. Sheets used: NDE Report (Weld Seam No, Method, Result, Report Date) and ITP Tracker (ITP No, Point, Inspection Status, Planned Date). (1) On a Rejects sheet list every row of the NDE Report whose Result is Reject, newest first, with the same headings. (2) In the ITP Tracker write Overdue in a Flag column for every point that is not Closed and is planned before today, and clear it on the others. (3) On a Method Summary sheet write, for each NDE method, how many results are Accept, Repair and Reject, methods A to Z. (4) On an Inspection Summary sheet write the number of rejects, overdue points and methods, with labels.
