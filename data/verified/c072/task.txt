An inspection follow-up tool, started with one macro. (1) On a Rejects sheet list every row of the NDE Report (Weld Seam No, Method, Result, Report Date) whose Result is Reject, newest first, with the same headings. (2) In the ITP Tracker (ITP No, Point, Inspection Status, Planned Date) write Overdue in a Flag column for every point that is not Closed and is planned before today, and clear it on the others. (3) On a Method Summary sheet write, for each NDE method, how many results are Accept, Repair and Reject, methods A to Z. (4) On an Inspection Summary sheet write the number of rejects, overdue points and methods, with labels. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
