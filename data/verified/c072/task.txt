A weekly shop tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Consumable Stock (Batch No, Qty On Hand, Reorder Level) write Reorder in a Remark column when Qty On Hand is below Reorder Level, and clear the Remark on the others. (2) On a Reorder List sheet copy those rows with their headings. (3) In the Calibration Tracker (Instrument ID, Next Calibration Due) highlight the Instrument ID cell red when overdue and yellow when due within 15 days, clearing the old fills first. (4) On a Weekly Summary sheet write the number of reorder lines, overdue instruments and instruments due soon, with labels. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
