Inspection follow-up tool, one macro. (1) Rejects sheet: rows of the NDE Report (Weld Seam No, Method, Result, Report Date) whose Result is Reject, newest first, same headings. (2) ITP Tracker (ITP No, Point, Inspection Status, Planned Date): Flag = Overdue where the point isn't Closed and is planned before today; clear Flag on the others. (3) Method Summary sheet: for each NDE method, how many results are Accept, Repair and Reject, methods A to Z. (4) Inspection Summary sheet: number of rejects, overdue points and methods, with labels. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
