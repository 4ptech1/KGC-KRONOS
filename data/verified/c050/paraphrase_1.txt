Monthly weld quality tool, one macro. (1) Weld Log (Weld Seam No, Welder ID, Weld Date, Process, Visual, Remark): Remark = Not qualified if the welder has no row for that process in Welder Quals (Welder ID, Process, Expiry Date) or the qualification expired before the weld date; clear Remark on the others. (2) Copy welds where Visual isn't Accepted or Remark is Not qualified to an Exceptions sheet, same headings. (3) Welder Summary sheet: each welder's welds, exceptions and exception rate. (4) Month Summary sheet: totals of welds, exceptions and rate. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
