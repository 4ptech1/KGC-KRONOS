A monthly weld quality tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Weld Log (Weld Seam No, Welder ID, Weld Date, Process, Visual, Remark) write Not qualified in Remark for welds whose welder has no row for that process in Welder Quals (Welder ID, Process, Expiry Date), or whose qualification expired before the weld date; clear Remark on the others. (2) Copy every weld whose Visual isn't Accepted or whose Remark is Not qualified to an Exceptions sheet with the same headings. (3) On a Welder Summary sheet write each welder's welds, exceptions and exception rate. (4) On a Month Summary sheet write the totals: welds, exceptions and rate. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
