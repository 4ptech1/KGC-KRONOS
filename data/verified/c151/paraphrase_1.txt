Procedure coverage tool, one macro to start. (1) WPS Register (WPS No, Process, PQR Ref, Remark): Remark = No PQR if PQR Ref is blank or not in the PQR List (PQR No, Process, Result), PQR failed if that PQR's Result is Fail, else cleared. (2) Welder Quals (Welder ID, Process, WPS Check): WPS Check = No WPS if the welder's process has no WPS without a remark, else cleared. (3) Process Coverage sheet: each process in the WPS Register with number of WPS, of WPS with a remark and of welders qualified for it, A to Z. (4) Report welder rows with No WPS. Output sheet rebuilt every run. Each numbered step also a macro on its own.
