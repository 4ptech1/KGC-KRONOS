Welding procedure check tool, one macro. (1) WPS Register (WPS No, Process, PQR Ref, Review Date, Remark): Remark = Check where PQR Ref is blank or Review Date is more than 365 days before today; clear Remark on the others. (2) No WPS sheet: every qualification in Welder Quals (Welder ID, Process, Expiry Date) whose Process has no WPS in the register (case doesn't matter). (3) Process Summary sheet: each process in the register with its number of WPS, how many are Check, and how many qualifications there are for it, A to Z. (4) WPS Summary sheet: total WPS, number of Check WPS and number of qualifications with no WPS, with labels. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
