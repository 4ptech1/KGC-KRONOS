Welding procedure check tool, started with one macro; the output sheets are rebuilt every run. Sheets used: WPS Register (WPS No, Process, PQR Ref, Review Date, Remark) and Welder Quals (Welder ID, Process, Expiry Date). (1) In the WPS Register write Check in Remark for every WPS whose PQR Ref is blank or whose Review Date is more than 365 days before today, and clear Remark on the others. (2) On a No WPS sheet list every qualification in Welder Quals whose Process has no WPS in the register (upper and lower case don't matter). (3) On a Process Summary sheet write each process in the register with its number of WPS, how many are Check, and how many qualifications there are for it, processes A to Z. (4) On a WPS Summary sheet write the total WPS, the number of Check WPS and the number of qualifications with no WPS, with labels.
