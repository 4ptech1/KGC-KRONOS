Receiving check tool, one macro. (1) GRN (GRN No, Part No, Heat No, Received Qty): Remark = Check when Received Qty isn't a positive number or Heat No is blank; clear Remark on the others. (2) Missing Certs sheet: GRN lines whose Heat No isn't in Material Certs (Heat No, MTC No). (3) Shortages sheet: each BOM (Part No, Required Qty) part whose total of the valid GRN quantities is below the required quantity, with the shortfall. (4) Receiving Summary sheet: number of Check lines, missing certs and short parts. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
