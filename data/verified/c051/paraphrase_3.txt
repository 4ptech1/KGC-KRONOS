Receiving check tool, started with one macro; the output sheets are rebuilt every run. Sheets used: GRN (GRN No, Part No, Heat No, Received Qty), Material Certs (Heat No, MTC No) and BOM (Part No, Required Qty). (1) In the GRN write Check in a Remark column when Received Qty is not a positive number or Heat No is blank, and clear Remark on the others. (2) On a Missing Certs sheet list the GRN lines whose Heat No isn't in Material Certs. (3) On a Shortages sheet list each BOM part whose total of the valid GRN quantities is below the required quantity, with the shortfall. (4) On a Receiving Summary sheet write the number of Check lines, missing certs and short parts.
