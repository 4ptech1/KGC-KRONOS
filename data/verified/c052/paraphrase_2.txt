I'd like a weekly QA report tool, started with one macro. First, an Overdue NCRs sheet listing the Open NCRs older than 30 days from the NCR Register (NCR No, Issue Date, Status, ITP No). Second, in the ITP Tracker (ITP No, Point, Inspection Status) write NCR Hold in a Hold Flag column for every point that has an Open NCR against its ITP No, and clear it on the others. Third, a Calibration Due sheet listing the instruments (Instrument ID, Next Calibration Due) that are overdue or due within 15 days. Fourth, a QA Summary sheet with labels and the number of overdue NCRs, held ITP points and instruments due. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
