Weekly QA report tool, started with one macro; the output sheets are rebuilt every run. Sheets used: NCR Register (NCR No, Issue Date, Status, ITP No), ITP Tracker (ITP No, Point, Inspection Status) and instruments (Instrument ID, Next Calibration Due). (1) On an Overdue NCRs sheet list the Open NCRs older than 30 days. (2) In the ITP Tracker write NCR Hold in a Hold Flag column for every point that has an Open NCR against its ITP No, and clear it on the others. (3) On a Calibration Due sheet list the instruments that are overdue or due within 15 days. (4) On a QA Summary sheet write the number of overdue NCRs, held ITP points and instruments due, with labels.
