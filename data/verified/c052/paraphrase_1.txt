Weekly QA report tool, one macro. (1) Overdue NCRs sheet: Open NCRs older than 30 days from the NCR Register (NCR No, Issue Date, Status, ITP No). (2) ITP Tracker (ITP No, Point, Inspection Status): Hold Flag = NCR Hold for every point that has an Open NCR against its ITP No; clear it on the others. (3) Calibration Due sheet: instruments (Instrument ID, Next Calibration Due) that are overdue or due within 15 days. (4) QA Summary sheet: number of overdue NCRs, held ITP points and instruments due, with labels. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
