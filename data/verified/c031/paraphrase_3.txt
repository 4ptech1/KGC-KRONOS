Weekly quality tool started with one macro, all three output sheets rebuilt every run: (1) on an Overdue NCRs sheet, list every Open NCR older than 30 days, taken from the NCR Register (columns NCR No, Issue Date, Status); (2) on a Calibration Due sheet, list every instrument due within 15 days or already overdue, taken from the Calibration Tracker (columns Instrument ID, Next Calibration Due); (3) on a Weekly Summary sheet, write both counts with labels.
