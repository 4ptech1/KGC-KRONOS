Filler traceability tool, one macro to start. (1) Weld Log (Weld Seam No, Weld Date, Batch No, Remark): Remark = Unknown batch if Batch No isn't in the Consumable Stock sheet (Batch No, Filler Class, Expiry Date), Expired batch if that batch expired before the Weld Date, else cleared. (2) Batch Usage sheet: each Batch No used in the Weld Log with its number of welds, most first. (3) Weld Problems sheet: welds with a remark, same headings. (4) Report welds with a remark. Batch numbers differ in case and stray spaces. Output sheets rebuilt every run. Each numbered step also a macro on its own.
