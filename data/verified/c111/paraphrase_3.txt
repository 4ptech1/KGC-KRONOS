For each line of the GRN sheet (GRN No, Delivery Note File, Copy Status): (1) I give the macro the Inbox folder and the GRN Archive folder, (2) make a folder named after the GRN No inside GRN Archive if it isn't there, (3) copy the Delivery Note File from Inbox into it, (4) write Copied, Already there, Missing or Bad name in Copy Status, (5) never overwrite, move or delete a file.
