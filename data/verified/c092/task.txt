A PQR follow-up tool, started with one macro. (1) In the WPS Register (WPS No, Process, PQR Ref, Remark) write PQR failed in Remark for every WPS whose PQR Ref is a PQR with the Result Fail in the PQR List (PQR No, Process, Test Date, Result), PQR missing when the PQR Ref is blank or not in the list, and clear Remark on the others. (2) On a Problem WPS sheet list the WPS that have a remark, with the same headings, WPS No A to Z. (3) On a Process Summary sheet write each process in the register with its number of WPS and how many of them have a remark, processes A to Z. (4) Tell me how many WPS have a remark. PQR numbers differ in case and stray spaces. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
