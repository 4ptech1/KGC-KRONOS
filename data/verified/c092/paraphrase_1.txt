PQR follow-up tool, one macro to start. (1) WPS Register (WPS No, Process, PQR Ref, Remark): Remark = PQR failed if PQR Ref is a PQR with Result Fail in the PQR List (PQR No, Process, Test Date, Result); PQR missing if PQR Ref is blank or not in the list; else cleared. (2) Problem WPS sheet: WPS with a remark, same headings, WPS No A to Z. (3) Process Summary sheet: each process in the register, number of WPS and how many have a remark, A to Z. (4) Report WPS with a remark. PQR numbers differ in case and stray spaces. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
