A consumables stock tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Consumable Stock sheet (Batch No, Filler Class, Qty On Hand, Reorder Level, Expiry Date, Remark) write Expired in Remark where the Expiry Date is before today, otherwise Reorder where Qty On Hand is at or below Reorder Level, and clear Remark on the others. (2) On a Reorder List sheet list the Reorder batches with the same headings, the lowest Qty On Hand first. (3) On a Class Summary sheet write each Filler Class with its total Qty On Hand, not counting expired batches, and its number of expired batches, classes A to Z. (4) Tell me how many batches are expired. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
