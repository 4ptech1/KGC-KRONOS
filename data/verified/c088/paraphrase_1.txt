Consumables stock tool, one macro to start. (1) Consumable Stock sheet (Batch No, Filler Class, Qty On Hand, Reorder Level, Expiry Date, Remark): Remark = Expired if Expiry Date is before today, else Reorder if Qty On Hand is at or below Reorder Level, else cleared. (2) Reorder List sheet: Reorder batches, same headings, lowest Qty On Hand first. (3) Class Summary sheet: each Filler Class, total Qty On Hand excluding expired batches, count of expired batches, A to Z. (4) Report expired batches. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
