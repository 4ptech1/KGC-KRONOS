Month-end dispatch report tool, one macro to start. (1) Dispatch sheet (Dispatch No, Job ID, Planned Date, Actual Date, Remark): Remark = Late if Actual Date is after Planned Date, Overdue if no Actual Date and Planned Date is before today, else cleared. (2) Job Tracker (Job ID, Customer, Status, Dispatch Status): Dispatch Status = Shipped late if the job has a Late dispatch, Shipped if it has any other dispatch with an Actual Date, Not shipped otherwise. (3) Late Dispatches sheet: Late and Overdue dispatches, same headings, earliest Planned Date first. (4) Customer Late sheet: each Customer with its number of Shipped late jobs, most first. (5) Report dispatches Overdue. Job IDs differ in case and stray spaces. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
