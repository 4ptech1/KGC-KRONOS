A month-end dispatch report tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Dispatch sheet (Dispatch No, Job ID, Planned Date, Actual Date, Remark) write Late in Remark when the Actual Date is after the Planned Date, Overdue when there is no Actual Date and the Planned Date is before today, and clear Remark on the others. (2) In the Job Tracker (Job ID, Customer, Status, Dispatch Status) write Shipped late when the job has a Late dispatch, Shipped when it has any other dispatch with an Actual Date, Not shipped otherwise. (3) On a Late Dispatches sheet list the Late and Overdue dispatches with the same headings, the earliest Planned Date first. (4) On a Customer Late sheet write each Customer with its number of Shipped late jobs, the most first. (5) Tell me how many dispatches are Overdue. Job IDs differ in case and stray spaces. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
