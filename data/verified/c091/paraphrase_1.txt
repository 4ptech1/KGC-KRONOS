Material certificate tool, one macro to start. (1) Material Certs sheet (Heat No, MTC No, Supplier, Grade, Remark): trim and upper-case every Heat No. (2) Remark = Check where MTC No or Grade is missing, else cleared. (3) Grade Summary sheet: each Grade with certificate count, A to Z; no grade counted under No grade. (4) Chase List sheet: each Supplier with at least one Check row, once, A to Z. (5) Report how many rows are Check. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
