A material certificate tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Material Certs sheet (Heat No, MTC No, Supplier, Grade, Remark) make every Heat No trimmed and upper case. (2) Write Check in Remark for every row with no MTC No or no Grade, and clear Remark on the others. (3) On a Grade Summary sheet write each Grade with its number of certificates, grades A to Z; rows with no grade are counted under No grade. (4) On a Chase List sheet write once each Supplier that has at least one Check row, A to Z. (5) Tell me how many rows are Check. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
