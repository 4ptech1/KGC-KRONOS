Supplier Receipts sheet, rebuilt every run, from the GRN sheet (GRN No, Supplier, Received Qty): (1) one row per supplier, with 'Acme Steel' and 'ACME STEEL ' treated as the same, (2) its number of GRN lines, (3) its total Received Qty, biggest first, (4) lines with no supplier are ignored, (5) a Received Qty that is not a number is left out of the total but the line still counts.
