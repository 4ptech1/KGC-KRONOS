From the GRN sheet (GRN No, Supplier, Received Qty) make a Supplier Receipts sheet with each supplier, its number of GRN lines and its total Received Qty, the biggest total first. 'Acme Steel' and 'ACME STEEL ' are the same supplier. Lines with no supplier are ignored, and a Received Qty that is not a number is left out of the total but the line still counts. The sheet is rebuilt every run.
