Calibration and NCR tool, one macro to start. (1) Calibration Tracker (Instrument ID, Type, Next Calibration Due, Status): Status = Overdue if the instrument is overdue, Due soon if due in the next 14 days, OK otherwise, No date if no due date. (2) NCR Register (NCR No, Instrument ID, Status, Cal Flag): Cal Flag = Overdue instrument for each NCR that is not Closed and names an Overdue instrument, Unknown instrument if the instrument isn't in the Calibration Tracker, else cleared. (3) Overdue Instruments sheet: the Overdue instruments, same headings, earliest due date first. (4) NCR Instruments sheet: each Instrument ID named in the NCR Register with its number of NCRs, most first. (5) Report NCRs flagged. Instrument IDs differ in case and stray spaces. Output sheets rebuilt every run.
