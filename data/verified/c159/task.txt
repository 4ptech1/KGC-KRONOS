A calibration and NCR tool, started with one macro. (1) In the Calibration Tracker (Instrument ID, Type, Next Calibration Due, Status) write Overdue in Status when the instrument is overdue, Due soon when it is due in the next 14 days, OK otherwise, and No date when there is no due date. (2) In the NCR Register (NCR No, Instrument ID, Status, Cal Flag) write Overdue instrument in Cal Flag for every NCR that is not Closed and names an Overdue instrument, Unknown instrument when the instrument isn't in the Calibration Tracker, and clear it on the others. (3) On an Overdue Instruments sheet list the Overdue instruments with the same headings, the earliest due date first. (4) On an NCR Instruments sheet write each Instrument ID named in the NCR Register with its number of NCRs, the most first. (5) Tell me how many NCRs I flagged. Instrument IDs differ in case and stray spaces. The output sheets are rebuilt every run.
